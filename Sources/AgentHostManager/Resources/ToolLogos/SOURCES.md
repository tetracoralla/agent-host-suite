## Interface fallbacks

`fallback-table.svg`, `fallback-file-search.svg`, `fallback-text.svg`, and
`fallback-layout-four.svg` are functional category glyphs, not product marks.
They are unmodified IconPark geometry rendered by Armorial with an outline
theme, 24 px size, and `#808584` stroke. IconPark is licensed under Apache-2.0;
see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. Installed or bundled product marks take precedence.
